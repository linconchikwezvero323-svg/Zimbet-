# ZimBet Brand Identity Guide

## Value Proposition
A mobile-first betting platform built for Zimbabwe. Fast, secure, and local.

## Visual Identity

### Logo
The ZimBet logo combines a stylized "Z" and "B" in the national colors of Zimbabwe, representing speed, agility, and local pride.
- **Icon**: `logo.svg`
- **Favicon**: `favicon.svg`

### Color Palette
- **ZimBet Green**: `#006837` (Primary) - Growth, landscape, and prosperity.
- **ZimBet Gold**: `#FBB03B` (Accent) - Wealth, excitement, and the Zimbabwe bird.
- **ZimBet Black**: `#1A1A1A` (Background/Text) - Strength and the people.
- **ZimBet White**: `#FFFFFF` (Surface) - Peace and clarity.
- **Status Green**: `#28A745` (Success)
- **Status Red**: `#DC3545` (Error/Danger)

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Inter`, `system-ui`, or `Roboto`
  - High legibility on mobile devices.
  - Low data footprint (standard system fonts).
- **Weights**:
  - Headings: Bold (700)
  - Subheadings: Semi-bold (600)
  - Body: Regular (400)

## Design Philosophy: Mobile-First & Low-Data
1. **Performance over Pixels**: Prioritize fast loading times. Use CSS effects and SVG icons over heavy raster images.
2. **High Contrast**: Ensure readability in bright sunlight (common in Zimbabwe).
3. **Optimized Touch Targets**: Buttons should be at least 44x44px for easy tapping.
4. **Minimalistic**: Clean layouts to reduce cognitive load and data usage.
5. **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Designed to feel like a native app while remaining light.
